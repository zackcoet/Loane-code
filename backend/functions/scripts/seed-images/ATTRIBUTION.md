# Seed photo credits

Placeholder photography for the local seed only. **Not for production
use** — before launch these are replaced by real students' own photos of
their own clothes.

the [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits free
commercial and non-commercial use without attribution. We credit anyway,
because the photographers did the work.

They are committed to the repo rather than fetched at seed time so the
seed works with no network, and stored as files rather than inlined so
the diff stays readable.
