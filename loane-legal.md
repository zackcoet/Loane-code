## 4. How Loane works

**Loane is a platform, not a party to your rental or sale.** Lenders and sellers own their items. Renters and buyers deal with them directly. Loane provides the app, payments, and tools to help, but we don't own, inspect, store, or deliver any item, and we don't guarantee any item's quality, fit, condition, or authenticity.

**Listings.** When you list an item, you confirm that:

- you own it and have the right to rent or sell it;
- your photos and description are accurate, including size, condition, and any flaws;
- the "documented value" you enter is an honest estimate of what it would cost to replace; and
- it's clean and ready to wear when handed over.

**Rentals.** A renter requests dates. The lender accepts or declines. The request expires if the lender doesn't answer within 48 hours or before the start date, whichever comes first. Once accepted, you arrange the handoff in the app. The lender records a condition photo at drop-off, the renter confirms she received it, and both confirm the return.

**Sales.** If an item is listed for sale, the buyer pays the listed price plus the service fee, and ownership passes to the buyer at handoff.

**Handoffs.** Handoffs happen in person between students. Loane doesn't arrange shipping or delivery. See Section 10 for safety.

## 5. Payments and fees

**Payments are processed by Stripe.** By paying or receiving money on Loane, you also agree to [Stripe's terms](https://stripe.com/legal), including the Stripe Connected Account Agreement for lenders. Loane never stores your full card number.

**Service fee.** Renters pay a Loane service fee of 15% on top of the rental price, with a $1.50 minimum. The fee is shown before you send a request. Lenders receive the full listed rental price.

**When you're charged.**

1. When you request a rental, we place a temporary hold on your card for the rental price plus fee.
2. You're charged when you confirm you've received the item.
3. If the request is declined, expires, or is cancelled before handoff, the hold is released.

**Payouts.** Lenders must set up payouts through Stripe before accepting paid rentals. The lender is paid after the rental is completed, once the return window in Section 7 has passed or the lender confirms the item came back fine.

**Beta period.** While Loane is in beta, some payments may be arranged directly between users. Loane isn't responsible for payments made outside the app.

## 6. Cancellations and refunds

| Who cancels, and when | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Renter, before the lender accepts | Nothing is charged. |
| Renter, 48+ hours before the start date | Full refund, including the service fee. |
| Renter, within 48 hours of the start date | Lender keeps 50% of the rental price. Service fee refunded. |
| Lender, at any time | Renter gets a full refund. The cancellation is recorded on the lender's account. |
| Lender never answers | Request expires after 48 hours. Nothing is charged. |

Repeated cancellations may limit your account.

## 7. Taking care of what you borrow

**Renters agree to:**

- return the item on time, in the same condition, unless the lender's listing allows otherwise;
- follow the lender's care notes (for example, "no washing or dry cleaning");
- not lend, resell, or alter the item.

**Return window.** After the renter returns an item, the lender has 48 hours to confirm it came back fine or report a problem. If she does neither, the rental completes automatically.

**If something goes wrong.** A lender can report damage, excessive cleaning needs, a missing item, or non-return within the 48-hour window, with photos. Loane reviews the drop-off photo, the return photos, messages, and both sides' accounts before deciding.

**Your liability as a renter.** Before you request a rental, you agree that if Loane decides you're responsible, your saved card may be charged for:

- reasonable cleaning or repair costs; or
- for loss, theft, non-return, or damage that can't be repaired, up to the item's documented value shown on the listing.

You'll be told the amount and the reason before you're charged. If a charge fails, your account may be paused until it's paid.

**Loane's decision.** Loane makes a good-faith decision based on the evidence available. Loane is not an insurer and doesn't guarantee that a lender will recover the full value of an item.
